Bitget lists tokenized US stocks that trade 24/7.

But Nasdaq shuts Friday 20:00 ET and reopens Monday 09:30.

For ~47 hours a week there is no external price for these assets anywhere.

I measured that window across 87 rTokens.

For large caps, ~100% of the price movement while the market is shut reverses once real liquidity returns. beta = -1.003, t = -3.46, 196 observations.

I call it Void Drift.
