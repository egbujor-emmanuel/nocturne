What this is. Bitget lists tokenized US equities that trade 24/7, but Nasdaq closes Friday 20:00 ET and reopens Monday 09:30 ET. For ~47 hours a week there is no external price for these assets anywhere — Bitget's internal matching engine is the only source, and it clears almost no volume.
Method. Fair value during a dark session is the last regular-session close. Validated walk-forward: it beats "the current price is right" by —% MAE on large caps, with —% directional accuracy. Depth, spread and the ±10% limit band are observed directly from the order book, not modelled.
What this is not. It is not a forecast of Monday's price. Fitted forecasting models were tested and lost to the baseline; only the unfitted full-fade model wins. The Noise Score ranks execution risk, it does not predict direction.
